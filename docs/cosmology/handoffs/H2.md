## Context

The program's spine: the new sector's linear perturbations evolve as spectators on a
CAMB-supplied ΛCDM background; observables come from propagation and conversion effects
on standard quanta. The ladder is O0 (pass-through validation) → O1 (recreate TorC
through our pipeline) → O2 (spectator perturbations; first: modified tensor propagation →
B-modes) → O3 (Gertsenshtein graviton↔photon on FRW; core goal — the thesis couplings) →
O4 (cosmic birefringence E→B, plus V-modes).

## Task

For **each rung O1–O4**, work out and report:

1. **What must exist** — the precise capability list (derivation-side, solver-side,
   observable-side, likelihood-side), mapped to the program's workstreams. Identify the
   *minimal* critical path per rung.
2. **Known-answer validation target** — the specific published result or limit the rung
   must reproduce (O1: TorC Zenodo chains; O2: GR limit → CAMB's tensor `C_ℓ^BB`, plus
   the Cembranos `ν`/`μ` phenomenology in `literature/2302.08186/`; O3: the flat-space
   Gertsenshtein `P = sin²(κB₀D/2)` limit as `H → 0` — see `docs/tex/gertsenshtein_formula.tex`
   for the project's verified formula and known literature sign/normalization traps;
   O4: the rotation formulae and current `β` measurements).
3. **Rough cost** — derivation time, per-likelihood-call cost, number of new components.
4. **What could invalidate the rung** — physics risks (e.g. O3: does a cosmological
   background B-field model have to be assumed, and which? O4: frequency scaling `ν⁰` vs
   `ν²` — **note this has no single answer**: the scaling is per-operator, and TIDAL's own
   theory documents do not derive it (`general_quadratic_lagrangian.tex` has no
   frequency-scaling discussion; its CS section states only that `χ^CS_2` sources magnetic
   helicity `A·B`). The Das et al. `ξ₁ ∇T×F̃` structure gives `α = 2ξ₁p²T₁t` (`ν²`) while an
   induced-axion `θFF̃` gives `ν⁰` — different sectors in the enumeration's language
   (`ζ̃₁₋₆` vs `d₁₈`/`χ^CS`). **Scope this as a per-operator derivation task, not a single
   number.** Background: `docs/references.md` §Cosmology Program (Das et al.,
   Eskilt & Komatsu rows); O2: is the PGT FRW background consistency check (`T̄ = 0`/tracking,
   `literature/2003.02690/`) satisfied for the theories we care about?).
5. **Data/likelihood availability** — which Cobaya likelihoods exist per rung; for O4
   note there is no drop-in birefringence likelihood (options: Gaussian prior on
   published `β`; fork `LilleJohs/cosmic-birefringence-planck-act` (MIT); SPT-3G BB lite
   for anisotropic).
